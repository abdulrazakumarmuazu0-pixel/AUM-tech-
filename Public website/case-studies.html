E-COMMERCE · RETAIL

Kasuwa Online — 60% Sales Growth in 3 Months

⚠ The Problem

A retail chain was losing customers to competitors with no online presence. Manual sales tracking caused constant stock errors.

⚙ Our Solution

Built a full e-commerce platform with Paystack payments, real-time inventory sync, seller dashboard, and WhatsApp order notifications.

✓ The Result

Online sales grew 60% in 3 months. Stock errors dropped to near zero. The client now processes 500+ orders monthly online.

GOVERNMENT · DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION

Hisbah Board Case Management System

⚠ The Problem

A government board tracked hundreds of complaints on paper — cases were lost, delayed, and impossible to audit.

⚙ Our Solution

Digitized the entire workflow: online complaint forms, automated case routing, staff dashboards, and audit logs with analytics.

✓ The Result

Case resolution time reduced by 70%. Full transparency achieved with searchable digital records. Citizen satisfaction significantly improved.

EDUCATION · E-LEARNING

Kalinfa Academy — LMS for 2,000+ Students

⚠ The Problem

An academy wanted to reach students across regions but had no way to deliver courses online or track progress.

⚙ Our Solution

Built a multi-language LMS (English/Hausa/Arabic RTL) with video streaming, downloadable PDFs, quizzes, and student analytics.

✓ The Result

2,000+ students enrolled in the first year. Course completion tracked automatically. Revenue from online courses grew 3x.
