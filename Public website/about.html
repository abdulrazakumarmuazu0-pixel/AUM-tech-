Our Story

From a Small Team to a Technology Powerhouse

AUM Technology Company was founded with a simple mission: to make world-class technology accessible to businesses of all sizes. Starting from Sokoto, Nigeria, we've grown into a trusted partner for organizations across multiple industries.

We believe technology should solve real problems. That's why every project we take on starts with understanding your business — not just your technical requirements.
